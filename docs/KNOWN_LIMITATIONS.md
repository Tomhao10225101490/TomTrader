# Known limitations

1. Keyless personal feeds now supply tested CN/HK/US quotes and history plus 22/26 global indices. No exchange-wide real-time entitlement or documented public-feed delay is established. Official Finnhub account validation remains pending; it is optional.
2. This local installation explicitly enables public sources for personal use; fresh checkouts require the environment switch. Yahoo returned 429 and is in backoff. Russell 2000/VIX/TOPIX/Nifty 50 and six trading FX quote instruments were unavailable in the clean run. Free endpoints can change or reject requests; no bypass is used.
3. No upstream WebSocket is configured. The app polls REST within host limits and distributes frames by SSE. Cached historical charts are not tick-by-tick charts; current candles are not synthesized from quote ticks, since they would misrepresent source OHLCV.
4. Company fundamentals/profile and 52-week alert conditions depend on a suitable provider. Missing values remain unavailable. The Finnhub adapter currently supplies quotes only.
5. Verified 2026 cash-market calendars cover the seven principal clock regions plus Paris/Amsterdam. Korea/Taiwan/India/Brazil/OTC and years outside verified coverage explicitly show unavailable; emergency closures and instrument halts need provider/calendar updates. Euronext exact year-end half-day closing times remain unverified.
6. All data is local to this one server installation. Preferences are per browser; lists/holdings/alerts are shared by clients of the same local server. No cloud sync, multi-user account or background push service is implemented.
7. Alerts require the local service and a visible app client, do not use stale prices, and cannot promise a notification while the page is closed/paused. Browser notifications depend on browser permission/platform support.
8. Portfolio P/L uses cost and market values translated at current reference FX; it does not reconstruct historical FX P/L, dividends, fees, tax lots or corporate-action transactions. Currency mismatch/missing FX excludes the position from totals and is displayed.
9. Heatmap pools are curated samples, not complete constituents. Equal-area sizing is used whenever full market-cap data is absent. Market movers are ranked within the same sample.
10. Real-mode screenshots demonstrate freshly fetched public-source data, closed-session cache and honest unavailable states. DEMO screenshots demonstrate interactions and must not be cited as proof of a live market feed. Performance measurements are local, cached HTTP/browser timings, not internet SLA/FPS claims.
11. Desktop Chrome is the browser automation target. Other browser engines, full screen-reader audits remain unverified. The keyless batch run tested the curated 132-instrument universe (including unsupported items), not a sustained session load or full market. Mobile pages are tested at 390×844 and additional target viewport sizes are recorded by the acceptance tool.
12. The Node SQLite API emits an experimental warning in Node 22. This is visible in verification logs; no native external DB dependency is required.
